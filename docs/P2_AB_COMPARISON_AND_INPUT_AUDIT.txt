Code evidence: p3/decoder.py:_log_emission sets the log-probability weight to cfg.missing_weight where ball position is absent. With missing_weight=0, all neural H/A/D evidence is discarded on those frames, even though P2 still has player-only features. This is loss of information AFTER P1. B raw correctly identifies 426/494 missing-position dead frames; decoded B identifies 0/494. Net B accuracy falls by 287 frames after decoding. This localizes the problem, but a controlled decoder ablation is still needed to separate effects of missing_weight, duration priors and temperature. The decoder also raises B spellF1 from 25.652% to 65.714%; it is a metric tradeoff, not uniformly harmful.

7. Speed-unit inconsistency: do NOT blindly divide Barca speeds by 3.6
XML labels scalar speed as speed_kph / kph, while repository DATA.md says m/s and the adapter passes it through unchanged. Empirical check from player positions strongly supports m/s-like values:
0.4-second central same-slot displacement check: 2579329 valid in-period comparisons; median derived_mps/recorded=0.999639; correlation=0.964159; median absolute error treating recorded values as m/s=0.114037m/s, versus dividing them by 3.6=1.125224m/s.
Other 0.08s, .96s and 2s windows show the same order of magnitude. This is a motion-based sanity check, not provider certification: short gaps, smoothing and track identity changes matter. The XML unit label appears inconsistent with numerical values. Document the mismatch and seek provider confirmation; do not silently apply an extra conversion. The check above concerns player speed, not certification of the intermittent ball-speed measurements.

8. Are we missing data or adding too much?
No evidence of an accidentally bloated feature tensor: 19 summaries plus flags is compact, and the 46 raw tracklet IDs are correctly not encoded as people. We have not run group-removal ablations, so do not claim 19 is optimal or that correlated features hurt.
Missing from the raw source: many ball positions, all public scalar ball speeds, some individual player speeds, reliable player identity, ball height/confidence. Missing by feature design: absolute ball location, pitch-boundary distances, velocity direction/closing motion, time since last valid ball observation, player-speed coverage counts, goalkeeper-role distinctions and explicit track-quality summaries. Some can be derived consistently; they are proposals, not already-tested improvements. Goalkeeper roles are available for two Barca slots but require a compatible IDSSE role mapping before any shared feature is added.
Available but intentionally excluded: Smart Tagging, homography and video are separate assets, not missing fields in the ATD parser. Homography may provide a quality signal; Smart Tagging is label-adjacent and not shared with IDSSE, so adding it is a new experiment requiring leakage/provenance review. Never add IDSSE BallPossession/BallStatus as features: they generate targets.
Potentially excessive observations, rather than excessive feature count: duplicate/extra tracklets and unreliable speed readings can distort the existing summaries. Do not cap counts at 11, drop distant players, clip the ball to the pitch or infer stable identities without a tested rule shared between providers.
